Connect Teams coming soon

A great idea needs the right mix of people. Connect Teams will match the roles a team needs with the skills members have.

What it will do

  • Match roles to skills. If a team needs a developer for its proof of concept, it will be linked to members who listed "developer", or something more specific like HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
  • Work both ways. Members see teams that need what they can do, and teams see members who fit.
  • Respect time. It takes account of the hours a week people say they can give.
  • Suggest, never decide. Idea owners choose who joins. Members choose what they join.

Get ready

The more complete your profile, the better the matches. Add the skills you can offer and how many hours a week you could give.
