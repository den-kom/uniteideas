Link Ideas coming soon

Many people have the same idea at the same time. Link Ideas will help them find each other instead of building the same thing twice.

What it will do

  • Spot overlap. It compares the categories, tags and public wording of one-pagers and shows you ideas that sit close to yours.
  • Suggest a link. If both authors agree, the ideas can be combined into one stronger idea with a shared team.
  • Spin off your own version. If you like part of an idea but want to take it a different way, you can start your own version that credits the original.
  • Keep sealed detail sealed. Matching only uses what is public. Nothing is linked or shown without the authors' say-so.

How it works

Simple, visible rules, not a black box: shared category, shared keywords and similar wording. A suggestion is only a suggestion. People make every decision.
