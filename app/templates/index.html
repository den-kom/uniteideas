How it works

  1. 1. Publish a one-pager

    A short public summary anyone can read and vote on. Keep the full details sealed until you are ready.

  2. 2. Verify your idea

    You get a receipt with the time you shared it. Anyone can check it, so the credit stays yours.

  3. 3. Build a team

    People offer the skills and hours they can give. You choose who joins.

  4. 4. Create a proof of concept

    Work together to show the idea can work, before anyone invests more time or money.
