How it works

Three steps. No jargon. Built so fear of being scooped does not kill the idea.

  1. 1. Publish a one-pager

    A short public summary anyone can read, back, and offer to help with.

  2. 2. Seal the details

    Keep the full write-up sealed. We publish only its fingerprint until you choose to reveal it.

  3. 3. Keep the receipt

    Download a proof bundle. Anyone can check the content and the time it was submitted.
