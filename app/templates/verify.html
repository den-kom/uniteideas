What this proves

When an idea is submitted, UniteIdeas computes a fingerprint of its content and sends only that fingerprint to public timestamp servers. They fold it into a batch that is written to the Bitcoin chain. Anyone can then check the fingerprint against the chain, without trusting us.

A timestamp is evidence that this exact content existed by that time. It is not a patent and it is not legal advice.

Check it yourself, without UniteIdeas

Download the timestamp file and the checked content from a verified idea. With the OpenTimestamps client run ots verify {id}.json.ots in the folder holding both files.
