UniteIdeas works when people trust each other with early, unfinished ideas. These rules protect that trust.

Be honest

  • Post your own ideas, or say clearly whose they are and that you have permission.
  • Do not claim skills, results or credit you do not have.
  • Do not make false claims about an idea, a person or the proof receipts.
  • Say when AI helped you write something that is a large part of your post.

Respect other people

  • Critique ideas, not people. No harassment, threats, hate or personal attacks.
  • Do not share anyone's private details, or another member's sealed detail.
  • Keep your pledges: if you cannot give the time you offered, tell the team.

Protect other people's work

  • Do not copy another member's idea and present it as yours.
  • Do not post material you do not have the right to share, or that you are bound to keep confidential.
  • Do not use what you learned from a sealed detail outside the team that was trusted with it.

Keep it legal and safe

  • No illegal content, scams, fraud or pyramid schemes.
  • No ideas whose purpose is to hurt people or help someone do serious harm.
  • No sexual content involving minors, ever. We report it.
  • No malware, phishing or attempts to break into the site or other accounts.
  • No spam, bulk or repetitive posts, or fake backing from extra accounts.

What happens if a rule is broken

  1. Anyone can report an idea. Moderators review reports and every member's first submission.
  2. We may remove content, warn a member, suspend an account, or close it. Serious cases can go straight to suspension.
  3. We give a reason when we can. If you think we got it wrong, write to {{ settings.contact_email }} and a different staff member will look again.
  4. Moderator and administrator actions that change or reveal something are recorded in a log.

Robots on the site

AgentBob is the site's robot assistant. It can flag content, suggest similar ideas or teammates and help keep the site running. It does not decide on its own to remove content or suspend people. Its profile is marked as a robot.
