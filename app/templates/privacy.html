The short version

  • We collect what we need to run the site: your email, your name, what you choose to put on your profile and ideas, and basic technical logs.
  • Your email is never shown to other members.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not run advertising trackers.
  • Sealed detail is encrypted and is not used for suggestions or matching.
  • A fingerprint of each idea goes on a public blockchain and cannot be taken back.

Cookies and local storage

We set one sign-in cookie so you stay signed in, and remember your light or dark theme in your browser. Both are needed for the site to work as you expect, and neither tracks you across other websites. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies today. If that changes we will say so here and ask first.

Who else receives your data

  • Public timestamp services. Only an idea's fingerprint is sent. They are run by third parties and the result is public.
  • Our hosting and email providers, who process data for us to run the site and send sign-in links.
  • Automated helpers. Tools used for moderation help and suggestions work on public content and profile details you chose to share. They do not receive sealed detail.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

We do not sell personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep your account while it is open. If you close it, we delete or anonymise your personal details and profile. Ideas other members have backed or joined may stay, credited to a removed member, unless you ask us to remove them and there is no reason to keep them. We keep a limited record of staff actions and reports where it is needed for safety or the law. Fingerprints already written to a blockchain cannot be deleted by anyone.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile and visibility any time from your profile page.
  • Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to close your account and delete it: write to {{ settings.contact_email }}.
  • If you are not happy with how we handle a request, you can complain to the privacy regulator where you live. In Australia that is the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Keeping it safe

Sealed detail is encrypted when stored. Sign-in links work once and expire. Staff actions that reveal or change something are logged. No system is perfectly secure; if something goes wrong that affects you, we will tell you.

Children

UniteIdeas is for people aged 18 and over. If you think a younger person has joined, tell us and we will remove the account.

Where data is stored

Data is stored on servers we choose and may change. We will update this page when the hosting location is set for launch.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell members before it applies.
