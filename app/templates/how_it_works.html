How UniteIdeas works

Good ideas die when their owner is afraid to share them. Here is how UniteIdeas takes that fear away, step by step.

  1. Publish a one-pager

    Write a short public page: the problem, who it helps, and roughly how it works. Pick a category and say whether it is digital or a physical product. People can read it, back it, and offer to help.

  2. Seal the details

    Put what you are not ready to share (dimensions, materials, algorithms, pricing, plans) in the sealed section. It is encrypted when stored. Only you and the teammates you approve can read it, until you decide to reveal it.

  3. Keep the receipt

    The moment you submit, you get a receipt: a fingerprint of your idea and the time. The fingerprint also goes to public timestamp servers that record it in the Bitcoin blockchain. Anyone can verify it later; nobody needs to trust us.

  4. Get votes

    Members vote on the ideas they want to see built. Votes decide what rises to the top, and show you where there is real interest.

  5. Build a team

    Members ask to join and say how many hours a week they can give. You choose who joins. Teams work out together who owns what they build.
