Unite Talent coming soon

A great idea needs the right mix of people. Unite Talent will suggest teammates whose skills and time fit the idea.

What it will do

  • Match skills to ideas. Using the skills members tick on their profile, it suggests people who could help with an idea, and ideas that could use a member's skills.
  • Respect time. It takes account of the hours a week people say they can give.
  • Suggest, never decide. Idea owners choose who joins. Members choose what they join.

Get ready

The more complete your profile, the better the suggestions. Add the skills you can offer and how many hours a week you could give.
