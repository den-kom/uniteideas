Unite Ideas coming soon

Many people have the same idea at the same time. Unite Ideas will help them find each other instead of building the same thing twice.

What it will do

  • Spot overlap. It reads the public one-pagers and notices when two or more ideas are close to each other.
  • Suggest a merge. If the authors agree, the ideas can be combined into one stronger idea with a shared team.
  • Spin off your own version. If you like part of an idea but want to take it a different way, you can start your own version that credits the original.
  • Keep sealed detail sealed. Matching only uses what is public. Nothing is merged or shown without the authors' say-so.

How it will work with robots

AgentBob, our robot assistant, will do the reading and suggesting. People make every decision: a suggestion is only a suggestion.
