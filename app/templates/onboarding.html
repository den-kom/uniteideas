Welcome to UniteIdeas

Most good ideas die because their owner is afraid someone will steal them. This platform is built to remove that fear, so ideas get built instead of forgotten.

  1. Submit a one-pager

    Write a short public description: the problem, who it helps, and roughly how it works. That page is your idea's home. It is public so people can find it, back it, and offer to help.

  2. Seal what you are not ready to share

    Put the full detail — dimensions, materials, algorithms, business model — in the sealed field. It is encrypted at rest. Only a fingerprint of it is published, which is enough to prove later that this exact text existed today.

  3. Keep your receipt

    Every submission gets a timestamped proof bundle you can download. Anyone can verify it on the verify page without seeing your sealed text. It is evidence of when you had the idea, not a patent.

  4. Your first post gets a quick review

    The very first idea you submit is held for a moderator to look at, which keeps spam off the site. Everything you submit after that goes live immediately. The timestamp is recorded the moment you submit, not when it is approved.

  5. Get votes

    Signed-in members can back an idea once. Backing decides what rises to the top of the feed and what gets attention from contributors.

  6. Build a team

    People ask to join and pledge the hours per week they can realistically give. You approve who joins. Pledges are capped at {{ settings.max_pledge_hours_per_week }}h/week across all projects so nobody over-commits.

  7. Find your people

    Your profile lists what you can help with. Soon UniteIdeas will suggest teammates who fit an idea, and ideas that overlap with yours, so good ideas find each other.
