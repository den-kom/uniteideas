1. What UniteIdeas is

UniteIdeas is a website where people share ideas, keep a timestamped record of when they shared them, find collaborators, and build early proofs of concept together. We run the website. We are not a party to any agreement between members, and we do not take part in building, funding or selling anyone's idea.

2. Your account

  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • You sign in with a one-time link sent to your email. Keep your email account secure; anyone who controls it can sign in as you.
  • Give a name that others can recognise. Do not pretend to be someone else.
  • One person, one account. Tell us if you think someone else has used yours.

3. Your ideas and who owns them

  • You keep ownership of what you post. We do not claim your ideas.
  • You give UniteIdeas a licence to store, display and deliver what you choose to make public (your public one-pager, profile and backing), so the website can work. For sealed detail the licence is limited to storing it securely and showing it to the people you allow.
  • Only post what you have the right to share. Do not post someone else's work, or information you are bound to keep confidential.
  • Public means public. Anything in your public one-pager can be read, copied and discussed by anyone. In many countries, publishing an idea can affect whether you can later patent it. If a patent matters to you, get professional advice before you publish. Putting detail in the sealed section does not file a patent.

4. Sealed detail

Sealed detail is encrypted when stored. Only these people can read it: you, members of the team you approve, and everyone once you choose to reveal it. Revealing is permanent.

UniteIdeas moderators and administrators can open sealed detail only to review a report or a first submission. Each time they do, it is recorded in a log that they cannot edit. We do not use sealed detail for suggestions or matching.

5. Proof receipts and timestamps

  • When you submit an idea we compute a fingerprint of its content and give you a receipt that records the time.
  • We also send the fingerprint, and only the fingerprint, to public timestamp services that record it in the Bitcoin blockchain. This record is public and permanent. It cannot be removed, even if you later delete your account or idea.
  • A receipt and timestamp are evidence that specific content existed by a certain time and was submitted from your account. They are not a patent, copyright registration, trade mark or any other legal right. Whether they help you in a dispute depends on the law where you are and the facts of your case. We make no promise that they will.

6. Working with other members

  • The person who posted an idea can approve who joins its team. Joining is a promise to try, not a job or a contract with UniteIdeas.
  • Members who work together should agree in writing who owns what they create, how credit is shared and what happens if someone leaves. UniteIdeas does not decide this for you. Disputes between members are between those members.
  • Pledged hours are a statement of intent. They are not a guarantee of work or payment.
  • Do not share another member's sealed detail or private messages outside the team.

7. Automated helpers

We use automated tools, including AI, to help with housekeeping: for example flagging content for moderators, suggesting similar ideas or possible teammates, and keeping the site running. Automated tools suggest; people decide about removals and suspensions. Suggestions can be wrong. Where the site shows content or a suggestion produced by an automated helper, it says so.

8. Rules of conduct

Follow the community rules. In short: be honest, be respectful, do not post anything illegal or harmful, and do not misuse other members' trust or the website.

9. When we step in

  • Any member can report an idea. Moderators review reports and first submissions.
  • We may remove content or suspend or close an account that breaks these terms or the rules, or that puts others at risk. We give a reason when we can.
  • If you think we got it wrong, write to us and we will look again.

10. What we do not promise

  • The website is provided as it is. We work to keep it running and your data safe, but we cannot promise it will always be available or error free.
  • We do not check ideas for originality, safety, legality or commercial value, and we do not promise that anyone will back, join or build your idea.
  • To the extent the law allows, UniteIdeas is not responsible for losses that come from what members post or do, or from relying on an idea, a receipt or a suggestion.
  • Nothing here limits rights you have under consumer law that cannot be excluded.

11. Leaving

You can stop using UniteIdeas at any time and ask us to close your account (see the privacy page). Terms that must continue to make sense after you leave, such as sections 3, 5 and 10, continue.

12. Changes

We may update these terms. If a change matters, we will tell members on the website or by email before it applies. Using the site after a change means you accept it.

13. Which law applies

These terms are governed by the laws of New South Wales, Australia, and disputes are for the courts of that state, unless the law of your country says otherwise.
